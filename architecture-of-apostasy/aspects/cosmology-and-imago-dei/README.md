# Cosmology and the Imago Dei

Develops the project's more mythic and cosmological material: creation order,
ancient rebellion, the inversion of the image of God, and the deeper conflict
behind institutional and embodied apostasy.

Initial questions:

1. How should cosmological claims be marked and restrained?
2. How does the Imago Dei become a target of inversion?
3. Which claims are biblical, historical, synthetic, or speculative?
4. How does Christ restore the image rather than merely defeat enemies?
