# Logos and Powers

Develops the project's account of Logos, created powers, principalities,
institutional embodiment, the fall of the powers, and Christ's disarming of them.

Initial questions:

1. What does Scripture mean by powers and principalities?
2. How do spiritual, symbolic, and institutional powers relate?
3. When does a created governor become self-referential?
4. How does Christ expose and restore power?
