# Architecture of Apostasy Aspects

This folder names major sub-aspects that should be developed under the main
Architecture of Apostasy project. Each aspect should eventually receive its own
roadmap, chapter list, source list, diagrams, and validation notes.

Every aspect remains accountable to `../grammar-of-apostasy/constitution.md`.

## Current Aspects

- Apostasy and Religious Systems
- Logos and Powers
- Fallen Liturgies
- Political Theology and War
- The Chemical Temple
- Sacred Time and Shadow
- Cosmology and the Imago Dei
- Visual Theology
- Restoration
