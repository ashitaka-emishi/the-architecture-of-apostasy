# The Grammar of Apostasy: Canonical Ontology

These are the controlling terms for the project. Essays may develop or qualify them,
but should not redefine them casually.

## Logos
God's ordering Word through whom creation exists.

## Principalities
Created spiritual governors intended to order creation toward God.

## Fall of the Powers
Principalities become self-referential and seek worship.

## Disease Logos
The false symbolic narrative proclaimed by fallen powers.

## Fallen Liturgy
Embodied rituals, symbols, habits, and institutions that incarnate the Disease Logos.

## Chemical Temple
The embodied human person—memory, attention, desire, neurochemistry, habits, emotions—formed by liturgy.

## Sacrifice
The human impulse toward self-offering. Good when directed toward God; distorted when redirected toward idols.

## Restoration
Christ disarms the powers, exposes false sacrifice, and reforms humanity through the Holy Spirit and the Church.
