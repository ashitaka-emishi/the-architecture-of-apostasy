## The Mark of the Troth

The Mark is borne by those who have freely undertaken the Rule.

It is not a sign of righteousness.

It is not proof of faithfulness.

It conveys no holiness, privilege, or authority in itself.

Its enclosure remains open because nothing entrusted to the keeper becomes the keeper's possession.

Its standing stroke signifies the guardian who remains at the place of responsibility.

Its empty place signifies the throne the guardian must never occupy.

The one who bears the Mark therefore does not declare:

**I am faithful.**

The bearer declares:

**I have consented to be judged by the keeping I have undertaken.**

The Mark belongs to the Order.

Vethra belongs to the Shepherd.

No human authority may confuse the two.

**Even the keeper must be kept.  
Nothing faithful keeps alone.**
