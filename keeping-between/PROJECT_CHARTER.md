# Project Charter

## Working title

**The Keeping Between Workbench**

## Purpose

Track the user's vision of the myth that is *Aru Va'en*: to understand it, place it in its proper place, and embody it faithfully, even if that embodiment becomes an order of one.

Create a traceable environment in which the following can develop together without collapsing into one undifferentiated body of claims:

1. *Aru Va'en* as mythic narrative;
2. the Doctrine of Unclaimed Virtue as constructive doctrine/discipline;
3. the Troth liturgical tradition;
4. biblical exegesis and canonical theology;
5. a proposed *Book of Wolves* structured around letters among wolves beneath the Shepherd;
6. authority, ecclesiology, repentance, reconciliation, mourning, and departure under the Troth;
7. visual and homiletical expressions.

## Governing research problem

How can a Christian fellowship remain faithful to Christ when authority, belonging, violence, suffering, moral identity, and even the claim to virtue can become mechanisms of self-justification or apostasy?

## Core answer under development

The project explores an answer with five coordinated claims:

- Christ is the measure.
- The Troth remains.
- Virtue is not ours to self-certify.
- Authority is entrusted responsibility under judgment.
- The Path remains open to repentance and return.

These are not all at the same evidentiary level. The workbench exists to keep those levels visible.
