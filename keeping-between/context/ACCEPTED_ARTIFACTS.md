# Accepted Artifacts

**Document status:** `working_synthesis`  
**Purpose:** human-readable index of project artifacts explicitly accepted as `user_established`

Acceptance records the user's theological and literary judgment within this project. It does not make an artifact Scripture, revelation, infallible doctrine, or immutable text. An accepted artifact may be reopened only through a new recorded decision and review proportionate to the proposed change.

Accepted artifacts remain in their subject folders under stable names. `source/` preserves frozen recovered evidence; `outputs/` is reserved for generated exports. The machine-readable status record remains `registers/artifact-register.json`.
