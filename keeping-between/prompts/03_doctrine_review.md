# Prompt — Adversarial Review of Unclaimed Virtue

Review `doctrine/Doctrine_of_Unclaimed_Virtue.md` and related proposition IDs.

Test for:
- relativism;
- moral paralysis;
- disguised self-righteousness (“I am virtuous because I do not claim virtue”);
- false equivalence between victim and aggressor;
- authority collapse;
- inability to name evil;
- cheap repentance;
- institutional evasion of responsibility;
- Christological vagueness.

For each failure mode, propose a bounded correction and identify whether it changes doctrine or only explanation.
