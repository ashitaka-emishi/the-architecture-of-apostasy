# Research Questions

## Troth / biblical theology

- What biblical categories best constrain the Troth without forcing lexical equivalence?
- What is the relationship among `pistis`, `alētheia`, `menō`, covenant faithfulness, and the project's “remaining” language?
- How should John 10 and Philippians 2 mutually constrain the model of authority?

## Unclaimed Virtue

- What are the strongest biblical warnings against self-righteous moral identity?
- How does the doctrine differ from humility, apophatic ethics, Lutheran simul iustus et peccator, and virtue epistemology?
- How can the doctrine avoid moral paralysis?
- What practices allow communities to test whether Unclaimed Virtue is actually operative?

## Authority

- How do Mark 10, John 13, Romans 13, Philippians 2, and pastoral epistles differ in their accounts of authority?
- What makes entrusted authority legitimate when the officeholder is fallible?
- What is the theology of resignation, removal, restitution, and restoration to office?

## Redemption of powers / fallen

- How do Colossians 1:15–20 and 2:15 constrain a theology of powers that can be reconciled yet disarmed?
- How should agent, office, and fallen configuration be distinguished?
- Can a person return while a harmful office/configuration must end?

## Liturgy

- What historical Christian call-and-response traditions offer useful analogies without implying false antiquity?
- How do corporate confession and institutional repentance work across Anglican, Lutheran, Reformed, Catholic, and Orthodox traditions?
