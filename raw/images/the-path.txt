Source note for gallery/the-path.png

Public asset:
gallery/the-path.png

Public use:
Home page, The Path of Re-Embodiment page, Images page, and Gallery Catalog.

Inspection note:
The image is a two-row path diagram titled "A Path of Deformation and Return."
It traces ten stages: false logos, false desire, repeated bodily practice,
spiritual deformation, institutional embodiment, sacrifice, suffering,
repentance, death to the false self, and re-embodiment in Christ.

Embedded Scripture references visible in the image include 2 Corinthians 11:4,
James 1:14-15, Proverbs 4:23, Romans 1:21-24, Matthew 23:13-15,
Romans 12:1, Romans 6:23, Acts 2:38, Romans 6:6, and 2 Corinthians 5:17.

Theological use:
Conceptual diagram and chapter/page illustration for the project's public path
of deformation and return.
