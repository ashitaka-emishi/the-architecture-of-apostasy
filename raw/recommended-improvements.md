# Recommended Improvements

Purpose: track improvements that strengthen the project’s sourcing, definitions, intellectual humility, reader orientation, document intake, AI/code discipline, and public site structure without allowing the archive itself to become a new Nehushtan.

## Foundational Frame

The project rests on two foundation documents:

1. **The Architecture of Apostasy**
   The diagnostic foundation: holy gifts, symbols, institutions, rituals, laws, and structures can become idols when detached from living dependence on God.

2. **Aru Va’en: A Keeping of the Troth**
   The embodied foundation: faithfulness is not mastery, purity, conquest, or detached analysis, but wounded keeping before the Shepherd, answering the cry without looking away.

Together they establish the governing rule:

> The archive must not merely diagnose apostasy; it must practice faithful keeping.

The website, code, AI system, and document intake process should therefore serve the path of return from false embodiment into Christ, not the enlargement of a theological monument.

---

## Summary

The project is rhetorically powerful, thematically coherent, visually strong, and increasingly well-structured as a Quarto archive. Its strongest insight remains that human beings can turn even God’s gifts into idols, and that repentance requires re-embodiment in Christ rather than mere institutional correction.

The next stage is not to weaken the prophetic vision, but to make it more durable: clearer intake rules, stronger sourcing, better claim classification, visible rival readings, pastoral restraint, faithful institutional examples, and code/AI systems that support theological judgment without replacing it.

---

## Core Guardrails

* Do not allow the website, archive, AI system, diagrams, code, or intake process to become a new Nehushtan.
* Preserve the distinction between public reading material, raw source material, canonical theological development, and AI/code implementation.
* Do not modify existing essays silently. Track essay-level recommendations before applying them.
* Recommended-improvements work must never touch essay content in `raw/` or `markdown/`. This document may only propose, track, and classify — actual edits to those directories require a separate, explicit request.
* Preserve the prophetic voice while adding humility, sourcing, proportion, and rival readings.
* Distinguish doctrine, exegesis, typology, analogy, historical judgment, prophetic warning, synthesis, and speculation.
* End canonical theological work with Christological resolution.
* Treat AI and code as servants of discernment, not authorities.
* Let the archive grow by faithful keeping, not by accumulation.

## Document Intake Process

The intake process should prevent raw accumulation from masquerading as faithful expansion.

### Proposed Intake Flow

```text
Raw source received
  ↓
Preserve faithfully in raw/
  ↓
Extract or convert to markdown/ when appropriate
  ↓
Classify the document
  ↓
Map it to canonical terms
  ↓
Identify source needs and rival readings
  ↓
Run mechanical checks
  ↓
Human editorial review
  ↓
Publish, revise, canonize, or hold in reserve
```

### Intake Questions

Every new document should answer:

* [ ] What kind of document is this?

  * Essay
  * Myth
  * Song
  * Image
  * Discussion
  * Source note
  * Prompt
  * Canonical chapter
  * Raw archive item
* [ ] What created good is being examined?
* [ ] What corruption, false logos, or fallen liturgy is being exposed?
* [ ] What cry is being heard?
* [ ] What wound does this document carry?
* [ ] What claim types are present?
* [ ] What evidence is needed?
* [ ] What rival reading should be considered?
* [ ] Does this belong in `raw/`, `markdown/`, `architecture-of-apostasy/`, `gallery/`, root `.qmd`, or `ai-system/`?
* [ ] Does it clarify the path of return, or merely enlarge the archive?
* [ ] Does it resolve in Christ, or only diagnose collapse?

### Intake Status Labels

Recommended labels for tracking new materials:

* `raw-preserved`
* `markdown-converted`

* `needs-citations`

## Acceptance Standard for New Public Work

A new public page, essay, image, song, or reading copy is ready when:

* [ ] It has a clear purpose.
* [ ] It belongs in the right repository layer.
* [ ] It identifies its claim type or genre.
* [ ] It does not confuse raw material with canonical theological development.
* [ ] It is linked from the proper archive or reading path.
* [ ] It does not merely expand the site but clarifies the path.
* [ ] It preserves humility where evidence is incomplete.
* [ ] It does not turn critique into contempt.
* [ ] It gestures toward Christ, restoration, repentance, or faithful keeping.

---

## Acceptance Standard for New Canonical Work

A new canonical chapter, diagram, prompt, schema, or roadmap item is ready when:

* [ ] It uses canonical terms consistently.
* [ ] It identifies the created good.
* [ ] It traces corruption without denying the goodness of creation.
* [ ] It names the relevant Disease Logos or false symbolic order.
* [ ] It identifies fallen liturgies and embodied formation.
* [ ] It identifies the sacrificial system.
* [ ] It distinguishes doctrine from synthesis.
* [ ] It cites sources proportionate to its claims.
* [ ] It includes objections or cautions.
* [ ] It resolves in Christ.
* [ ] It passes automated checks where applicable.
* [ ] It has been reviewed by a human editor.
