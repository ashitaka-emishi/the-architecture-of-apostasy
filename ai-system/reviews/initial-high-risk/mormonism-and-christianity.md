## Summary

The current essay functions as a compact source dossier: it collects LDS canonical and early-leader statements about traditional Christian creeds, churches, apostasy, and exclusive authority. Its basic direction is clear, but the public-facing risk is that the document presently supplies severe quotations with minimal sourcing apparatus, little historical context, and almost no pastoral distinction between critiquing doctrine and addressing LDS people. A reader could experience the piece as a blunt adversarial exhibit rather than a careful theological boundary-setting essay.

Recommended status remains Needs Source Strengthening and Needs Pastoral Review. A later document-change pass should verify every quotation against primary sources, add locators and dates, distinguish LDS institutional claims from LDS persons, and frame the purpose as doctrinal clarity rather than contempt.
