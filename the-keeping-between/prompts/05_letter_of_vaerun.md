# Prompt — Letter of Vaerun

Read the letter design constraints, recipient register, relevant Aru Va'en scenes, doctrine, and biblical dossier.

Before drafting, answer:
- When is this letter written?
- Why can this recipient receive it under current narrative chronology?
- What concrete problem prompted it?
- How is Vaerun implicated in the same danger?
- What correction could the recipient rightly offer Vaerun?
- Which claims are biblical versus constructive?

Then draft in an apostolic-pastoral voice without imitating KJV diction or claiming Scripture-status.
