# Provisional Keeping Between Drafts

**Status:** `proposed`

No order has been formed or established. These documents no longer propose the older recovered order-name. They explore what disciplined form might be required if the vocation now named **The Keeping Between** continues to be born as a provisional order.

The Keeping Between is not the name of a permanent religious body. It names a vocation for an age of passage, when keeping may require neither preserving the old world nor enthroning the new one. If it becomes an order at all, it remains provisional and may pass away when its charge is complete.

`KEEPING_BETWEEN.md` records the concise embodied-vocation statement. The constitution, rule, short rule, and sigil files remain conditional design drafts downstream from that statement.

The drafts are downstream from *Aru Va'en*, not authorities over it. The myth remains the project's central symbolic narrative, and the Shepherd remains its Christological center and measure. Disciplined-form language may apply the myth but may not redefine its offices, symbols, or theology.

## Controlling office vocabulary

- **Vaelun:** threshold or boundary calling; discernment at places of crossing.
- **Vaerun:** crossing calling; one set at the veil and bound to cross when fidelity requires it.
- **Hrovan:** hall-bearing or center-bearing calling; responsibility near communal and concentrated authority.
- **Keeper:** a general description for anyone entrusted with responsibility, not a distinct rank.

These are different kinds of calling, not stages in a ladder. A change of calling is neither promotion nor demotion. `Hrovann` in the recovered institutional sources is a misspelling of `Hrovan`.
