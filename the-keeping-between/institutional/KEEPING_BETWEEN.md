# The Keeping Between

There are times when keeping requires building.

There are times when keeping requires teaching.

And there are times when the structures themselves are passing away.

In such an age, some are called neither to preserve the old world nor to enthrone the new one, but to keep faith through the passage between them.

This is the Keeping Between.

It claims no permanence for itself.

It possesses no virtue of its own.

It does not presume to know what form the world must take.

Its charge is smaller and harder:

> hear what must not be forgotten,  
> guard what must not be surrendered,  
> discern what is being born,  
> and remain until the passage is complete.

Then the Keeping may pass away.

The Troth remains.

## Status Guardrails

No order has been formed or established. This document names an embodied provisional vocation and possible disciplined structure, not a permanent institution, settled rule, membership body, or authority.

If The Keeping Between becomes an order at all, it remains provisional, accountable, and capable of passing away when its charge is complete. It may never claim possession of the Troth, the myth, virtue, Christ's authority, or the right to determine the future form of the world.
