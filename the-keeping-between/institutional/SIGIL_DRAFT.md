## The Sigil of the Troth

**Status:** `proposed`

No order has been formed, and this Sigil has not been adopted. If The Keeping Between were formed as an order, the Sigil could be used only within the voluntary practice of those who freely undertook its Rule.

Recovered source image, original filename retained: `../source/institutional/mark.png`

It is not a sign of righteousness.

It is not proof of faithfulness.

It conveys no holiness, privilege, or authority in itself.

Its enclosure remains open because nothing entrusted to the keeper becomes the keeper's possession.

Its standing stroke signifies the guardian who remains at the place of responsibility.

Its empty place signifies the throne the guardian must never occupy.

The one who uses the Sigil therefore does not declare:

**I am faithful.**

The bearer declares:

**I have consented to be judged by the keeping I have undertaken.**

The proposed Sigil would belong to the voluntary practice of The Keeping Between, if such practice is ever adopted, not to any participant as a possession.

The frozen source calls this graphic the Mark. Active project vocabulary calls it the Sigil so that `mark` may describe the subtle testimony of Vethra without institutional confusion.

Vethra belongs to the Shepherd.

No human authority may confuse the two.

**Even the keeper must be kept.<br>
Nothing faithful keeps alone.**
