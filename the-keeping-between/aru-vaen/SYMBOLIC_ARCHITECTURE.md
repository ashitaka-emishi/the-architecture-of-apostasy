# Aru Va'en — Symbolic Architecture

This is a working interpretive map, not a replacement for the source.

| Truvane | gathered faithful / unreleased fellowship | pasture claims exclusive righteousness |

## Governing inversion

The myth begins with an apparent task of the wolves keeping the vulnerable. Its mature form should reveal a deeper dependency:

**the Shepherd keeps the keepers.**

He sorts within bodies rather than between persons. He separates without severing, restoring members caught in the four curses to the body and the body to right relation beneath himself. The wolves mirror this at the circumference by making room between bodies without destroying the bodies they resist.

Therefore no keeping is solitary, self-originating, or self-justifying.

No body is a fixed moral species. `Assailant`, `afflicted`, and `fallen` name present action or condition, while the Black Sun's near-likenesses are unstable manifestations rather than a disposable race. The narrative language must change when action and relation change.

Aruvan and the Living Lamb are two faces of one creaturely communion beneath the Shepherd: keeping and life kept, restored, gathered, and becoming companion. They are not separate beings that complete, save, possess, or judge one another. The Shepherd precedes, judges, gathers, and keeps the whole relation.

Aruvan's redemption is relinquishment without annihilation. Its closed body loosens; what had gathered into stature returns to the living as accountable memory, responsibility, practice, and distinct callings. The relation remains, but the self-contained corporate body does not.

The recovered institutional Mark remains a provisional proposal under D-022; no Order has been formed and the graphic may never be adopted. D-054 renames it the Sigil in active vocabulary. Its most important present contribution to the myth is negative: **the Sigil is not Vethra.**

Vethra is borne distinctly by every companion who passed through the Keeping, including Harrow's dead flesh. It is perceived chiefly but fallibly by Gravane and Truvane and makes familiar flesh seem almost `off` because received relation remains present. This is the inverse of the fallen near-likeness, whose almost-right surface conceals relation imitated, displaced, or missing. Physical wounds may accompany Vethra but never constitute it.

The source calls `Vaelun` coal-under-ash and unfolds `Vey` from small, through ember, to the last light under ash. Vethra shares this symbolic grammar as hidden first light of eschatological fullness in Christ. It carries the costliness of remaining, restraint, relinquishment, grief, correction, responsibility, and vulnerability without becoming payment or reward. Harrow bears it without animation or anticipated resurrection. The whole company displays it through the accepted communal frame; Morning then pauses only over Harrow and Ostra. Murn and Keld remain bearers without separate portraits, Vaerun does not certify his own Vethra, and Vey displays his through embodied presence beside Vaerun.

Morning's shared meal is a firstfruits sign, not the final Feast: bread eaten in promise after the wounded and dead have been tended. It images Hrovan, Vaelun, Vaerun, Gravane, Truvane, Lamb and flock, Vethra, and Troth in proper relational order beneath the Shepherd without becoming a seating hierarchy. The defensive circle becomes an open ring with several passages and no head table. The Shepherd orders the sharing, but the food does not save, erase accountability, restore office, or fill Harrow's absent place. Hrovan pours for the wounded; Gravane attend to memory and need; Truvane distribute; Vaelun keep open passages and rotate into the meal; Vaerun carries provision across the former boundary and returns among his companions; Lamb and flock participate actively. Vethra is borne in flesh rather than displayed, and the Troth remains through enacted fidelity rather than appearing as a guest, vessel, food, or power beside Christ. Fang and fleece eat as fellow creatures beneath the Shepherd; no creature or lamb is sacrificed to supply the table.
