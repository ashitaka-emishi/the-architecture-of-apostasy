## Christological Audit

1. **Christ remains the measure.** The Shepherd, not Troth, office, circle, suffering, or Vethra, performs the personal acts of knowing, calling, healing, judging, receiving, giving thanks, and gathering.
2. **The opening is governed by the ending.** The invocation's primordial Troth language is not left alone: **"Before the Troth, there was the Shepherd"** and **"Before grief was love"** establish personal and Christological priority.
3. **The Shepherd is recognizable without a superscription.** His wounded human body, scars received before the hill, torn robe, knowing call, non-grasping care, truthful grief, and opened hands carry the identity before Vey names his priority.
4. **Shepherd and creaturely lamb remain distinct.** The lamb receives care, remembers, chooses movement, accompanies a returning sheep, eats, and rests beneath a scarred hand. It does not atone, judge, command, or become Christ.
5. **Grace precedes creaturely response.** Healing begins before the lamb can respond, and the returning sheep hears a name it did not create. Creaturely participation is real without becoming self-salvation.
6. **No creaturely death repeats the cross.** Harrow's death, the lamb's wounds, Vaerun's wound, Vethra, and the meal purchase nothing. No lamb is killed for the meal.
7. **Aruvan relinquishes beneath Christ.** The long wolf bows, opens once, and yields accumulated memory and responsibility. No ancestor, hidden hall, or institution remains as an independent saving body.
8. **The omitted source line remains correctly omitted.** Removing **"We were what remained after the Troth had passed through flesh"** prevents the Troth from sounding like the incarnating subject while the revised Vethra imagery preserves costly embodied testimony.

**Christological conclusion:** Pass. The remaining risks are risks of reader overinterpretation, not contradictory claims in the candidate.

The progression is coherent and cumulative. Later Keepings answer rather than merely repeat earlier claims: Night's stone seat is answered by the Hrovan's confession; Vaerun's fear of insufficiency is answered by **"None of us is the measure"** and by Vey's embodied support; the cry becomes the Shepherd's naming call; fang moves from threatened violence to bounded protection and finally cradle.

### Voice and register

- The first-person retrospective voice remains stable, with gnomic statements used as liturgical compression rather than a change of narrator.
- The old oral register survives through short clauses, elemental scent and texture, ritual responses, triads, negation, and controlled repetition.
- Exact `old` appears only three times. Exact `fear` is absent; its few cognates occur where fear is narratively concrete rather than clustered as opening explanation.
- `Cry`, `Shepherd`, `remain`, and `open` function as governing motifs. Their repetitions develop from interruption to naming, possession to relinquishment, closed violence to opened relation, and ordeal to promise.
- Spoken responses remain limited. Harrow, Murn, Sable, Little Vey, the refusal, the communal objection, the Hrovan's confession, and the final line retain distinct weight without turning the myth into dialogue exposition.
- Word distribution is proportionate to narrative burden: Night 641; Pre-Dawn 1,091; Calling 1,225; Eternal Dawn 2,467; Mourn 1,472.

### Literary clarity resolution

D-087 replaces the ambiguous figure with **one of the host** and shows its present action: it strikes the Shepherd with its mouth closed around its own cry. He has lowered the lamb beside his knee, opens both hands, gives ground beneath the host member's weight, and does not throw it back. The following line explicitly identifies the lamb and the Shepherd as the pair separated by the press.

The revision preserves personhood through `host`, identifies assault through action rather than permanent taxonomy, and makes the Shepherd's nonviolence visible against the bounded throwing and restraint used by the wolves.

**Literary conclusion:** Pass. The D-087 clarification is accepted under D-088.
