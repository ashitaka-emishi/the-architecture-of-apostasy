# A Lament for JoJo
## Keeping, Remaining, and Release

O Christ, Shepherd of all that breathes,
hear us now for JoJo,
for those who kept him,
and for those who remain.

**The Troth remains.**

The Father remains faithful.

The Son reveals the Father
and keeps what the Father gives him.

The Spirit teaches us to cry to God
when our own words fail.

Love remains.

Therefore the Troth remains,
not because our keeping cannot fail,
but because your faithfulness does not fail.

Therefore faith remains possible,
even when our keeping fails.

Before we knew how to keep him,
before his feet had marked our days,
JoJo chose Lucy.

He knew his person.

And Lucy received what was entrusted to her.

From that first bond
the keeping widened.

Angie gave him motherhood.

Wyatt gave him brotherhood.

Henry loved him more quietly,
and quieter love was still love.

I was his friend.

I walked and played with him
beside his brothers,
Otis and Pumpernickle.

Otis and Pumpernickle kept him
as brothers keep one another,
with bodies,
with nearness,
with shared places
and familiar days.

Each member of the household kept him,
human and creature,
and each was drawn into keeping with him
according to the love given.

Different loves gathered around one creature,
and JoJo received each
according to its kind.

Remember JoJo.

Remember the paths beneath his feet,
the games,
the walks,
his brothers beside him,
and the sound of his greeting
when someone he loved came home.

Remember Lucy,
whom he chose.

Remain with Lucy,
who received his choosing
and bore the first wound of his leaving.

Keep her grief
without asking it to become smaller
than her love.

Let what JoJo gave her
remain true in her
without binding her to his absence.

Remember the ordinary days,
whose holiness is seen more clearly
now that their number has ended.

And remember where those days were given.

For JoJo walked among us
as a living sign of covenant
between this family and the Susquehanna.

He placed his feet upon this land,
breathed its seasons,
and walked beside its waters.

If the Susquehanna bears witness,
let it bear witness as creation,
not as another god,
but as the place where your creature lived
and the ground that received his feet.

For you entered creation yourself.

You did not despise
matter, flesh, body, creature, land, or history.

You remain faithful within it.

Then sickness entered JoJo.

Hidden within his head,
a great tumor grew.

For three months
his body carried
what none of us could see.

And when Lucy and Angie
brought him to the doctor,
they wondered that he was alive.

More than that,
he could still walk.

JoJo remained.

He kept going
far beyond what his body
should have been able to bear.

For his heart was far larger
than the body that contained it.

But remaining is not the same
as being commanded to remain forever.

There comes a threshold
where holding on becomes refusal.

There comes a threshold
where keeping becomes release.

And here I confess:

**I was never his Shepherd.**

I was only given
a little while to keep him.

I could walk beside him.

I could play with him.

I could feed him.

I could seek help
when sickness came.

I could love him.

But I could not command life to remain.

I could not heal
what I did not understand.

I could not cross
the final threshold in his place.

**I was his keeper.**

**I was never his Shepherd.**

So I stand now
as a keeper beneath judgment.

Judge my keeping, O Shepherd.

Where I was faithful,
let me claim no virtue.

Where I failed him,
show me truth without excuse.

Where I could do nothing,
teach me not to pretend
that helplessness was mastery.

This is the center of my confession:

I kept what I could.

I release what I cannot.

You are the Shepherd.

From that confession,
let my witness be rightly ordered.

Concerning JoJo,
I do not seize the Judge's chair.

I stand as witness.

I stand for the creature
who cannot speak his testimony
in human words.

And my witness widens
to those who kept him:

to Lucy's received choosing,
Angie's motherhood,
Wyatt's brotherhood,
Henry's quieter love,
and the whole family's participation
in the Troth,
not as proof that we were righteous,
but as the true keeping
entrusted to us beneath Christ.

So hear my testimony, Shepherd:

**JoJo kept what was given to him.**

And this family kept him together,
each according to the love given:

Lucy as the one he chose,

Angie as mother,

Wyatt as brother,

Henry in his quieter way.

Their keeping participated in the Troth,
not by proving us righteous,
but by answering Christ's keeping
with entrusted love.

Death does not erase
the faithfulness of life.

It does not make the walks untrue.

It does not undo
the welcome at the door.

It does not make false
the years in which JoJo knew Lucy
as his person.

It does not erase Angie's motherhood,
Wyatt's brotherhood,
Henry's quieter affection,
his brothers,
the land beneath his feet,
or the river beside him.

The Susquehanna may bear witness,
but it cannot hold him forever
upon her banks.

So, Shepherd,

**keep what we cannot keep.**

Where our hands end,
let yours continue;
where our knowledge fails,
let your wisdom remain.

**The keeping was true
because at last it had to be released.**

**The love was true
because releasing him cost us something.**

The pain does not prove
that love failed.

The pain is there
because something true
has passed from our hands.

So none of us can keep JoJo now.

Yet those who kept him
still remain before you.

When Lucy grieves
for the creature who chose her,
remain with her.

When Angie, Wyatt, and Henry grieve,
remain with them.

When Otis and Pumpernickle
search familiar places
for the brother who does not return,
have mercy upon creatures
who understand absence
without having words for death.

And keep us as a family
beneath your keeping.

Each of us kept JoJo
according to the love given to us.

Each of us is kept by you now.

And when I turn from JoJo's absence
toward those still entrusted to me,
keep my keeping faithful.

I say my family
not because they belong to me,
but because I belong to them.

Let my fatherhood
and my patriarchal charge
serve their grief
without possessing it,
let me bear responsibility
without making me the measure,
and let me remain faithful
without becoming a throne.

A keeper is not commanded
to prevent every ending.

A keeper is commanded
to remain faithful
to what has been entrusted
for as long as it is entrusted.

And when the hour comes,
to release it.

For no creature
was given to me as possession.

JoJo was entrusted.

Nothing faithful keeps alone.

**Even the keeper must be kept.**

We are not what we were
before this trial.

We are not what grief
sought to make us.

In Christ's keeping,
we are remembered into ourselves:

not untouched,
not destroyed,
but held through grief,
marked in flesh,
and returned to faithful keeping.

JoJo has passed
beyond the reach of my keeping.

Therefore I entrust him
to the Shepherd whom I am not.

For Lucy,
JoJo's person,
chosen and wounded by love.

For Angie, Wyatt, and Henry,
who kept him according to their loves.

For Otis and Pumpernickle,
his brothers.

For this family,
standing together beneath your keeping.

For the witness I bear
not only to JoJo,
but to the keeping of this family:
let it be received, corrected, and completed
by Christ the Shepherd.

For the Susquehanna,
for the Spirit who gives life,
for the keeper who releases
what he cannot keep,
and for JoJo.

What we kept was never ours to possess.

What we release is not abandoned.

What has passed beyond our keeping
has not passed beyond yours.

**Christ remains.**

**Love remains.**

And because Christ remains,

**The Troth remains.**

Amen.
