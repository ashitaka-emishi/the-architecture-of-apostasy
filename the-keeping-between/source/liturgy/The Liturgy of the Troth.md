# The Liturgy of the Troth

*A brief office of gathering, repentance, hearing, and sending*

## I. Gathering

The people stand.

The Presider faces the assembly but does not ascend above them.

**Presider:**  
Before throne and pasture.

**People:**  
Before fang and fleece.

**All:**  
**The Troth remains!**

A silence is kept.

**Presider:**  
Before us were truth, mercy, and the call of God.  
After us they shall remain.

We have not gathered because we are righteous.

**People:**  
We have gathered because we are called.

**Presider:**  
We have not gathered because we bear the sign.

**People:**  
We have gathered that we may learn again to hear.

---

# II. The Naming

The people remain standing.

**Reader:**  
The throne may command.

**People:**  
But the throne is not the measure.

**Reader:**  
The pasture may shelter.

**People:**  
But the pasture is not the measure.

**Reader:**  
The fang may wound.

**People:**  
But the fang is not the measure.

**Reader:**  
The fleece may suffer.

**People:**  
But the fleece is not the measure.

**All:**  
**The Troth remains.**

---

# III. Confession

The people may kneel or bow.

**Presider:**  
Let each ask not first,  
*Have I been good?*

Let each ask:

*What cry have I trained myself not to hear?*

A long silence is kept.

**Presider:**  
Where we made obedience greater than truth—

**People:**  
Call us back.

**Presider:**  
Where we made belonging greater than mercy—

**People:**  
Call us back.

**Presider:**  
Where we named another wolf so that we might devour without shame—

**People:**  
Call us back.

**Presider:**  
Where suffering persuaded us that everything we desired was righteous—

**People:**  
Call us back.

**Presider:**  
Where virtue became our possession and righteousness became our name—

**People:**  
Strip the name from us.

**Presider:**  
Where we heard the cry and taught ourselves not to hear—

**People:**  
Give us ears again.

A silence is kept.

---

# IV. The Greeting of Repentance

When appropriate, the Presider says:

**Presider:**  
Who among us has remained without failure?

No answer is given.

Then:

**Penitent / People:**  
I did not remain.

**Presider:**  
You are not the measure.

**All:**  
**The Troth remains.**

**Presider:**  
Therefore confession is possible.

**People:**  
Therefore return is possible.

**Presider:**  
Therefore restitution is required where restitution can be made.

**People:**  
Therefore mercy shall not become a lie.

---

# V. The Hearing of the Word

A reading from Scripture follows.

Suitable readings include:

- Micah 6:6–8
- Isaiah 58:6–12
- Ezekiel 34:1–16
- Matthew 25:31–46
- Mark 12:28–34
- Luke 10:25–37
- John 1:1–14
- John 10:1–18
- John 13:1–17
- Romans 12:1–21
- Philippians 2:1–11
- 1 John 4:7–21

After the reading:

**Reader:**  
Hear what the Spirit says.

**People:**  
Let hearing become flesh.

A silence follows.

---

# VI. Examination

The Presider asks slowly:

**Presider:**  
Where is the throne in you?

Silence.

Where is the pasture you fear to leave?

Silence.

Whom have you named wolf?

Silence.

Where have you wrapped yourself in fleece?

Silence.

Whose cry have you made into noise?

A longer silence.

**Presider:**  
Do not answer quickly.

**People:**  
The Path is walked before it is named.

---

# VII. The Peace of the Troth

The people rise.

Before exchanging peace, all say:

**First side:**  
Before throne and pasture.

**Second side:**  
Before fang and fleece.

**All:**  
**The Troth remains!**

Members may then greet one another.

Where reconciliation is specifically needed, two persons may use:

**One:**  
What passed between us is known.

**Other:**  
What was broken is not forgotten.

**Both:**  
**Yet the Troth remains.**

Reconciliation shall not be demanded where truth has not yet been spoken or where safety requires separation.

---

# VIII. Intercession

**Presider:**  
For those beneath unjust thrones—

**People:**  
May their cry be heard.

**Presider:**  
For those cast from the pasture—

**People:**  
May their cry be heard.

**Presider:**  
For those wounded by the fang—

**People:**  
May their cry be heard.

**Presider:**  
For those becoming what they once feared—

**People:**  
May their cry be heard.

**Presider:**  
For rulers, shepherds, judges, teachers, parents, and all entrusted with another—

**People:**  
Keep them beneath the judgment of mercy.

**Presider:**  
For enemies—

A silence is kept.

**People:**  
Do not permit us to make their humanity disappear.

**Presider:**  
For ourselves—

**People:**  
Do not permit us to mistake ourselves for the Good.

---

# IX. The Common Troth

The assembly stands together.

**Presider:**  
What remains when the throne falls?

**People:**  
The Troth remains.

**Presider:**  
What remains when the pasture burns?

**People:**  
The Troth remains.

**Presider:**  
What remains when the wolf repents?

**People:**  
The Troth remains.

**Presider:**  
What remains when the sheep becomes wolf?

**People:**  
The Troth remains.

**Presider:**  
What remains when we discover that we were wrong?

**People:**  
The Troth remains.

**Presider:**  
What remains when we return?

**All:**  
**The Troth remains!**

---

# X. Commitment

**Presider:**  
Then let the Troth become flesh among us.

**People:**  
In speech.

**Presider:**  
In mercy.

**People:**  
In repentance.

**Presider:**  
In endurance.

**People:**  
In service.

**Presider:**  
And when another cries—

**People:**  
We will not make the cry into noise.

---

# XI. Sending

Those departing may exchange the Greeting of Departure.

**Presider:**  
Though the road divide us.

**People:**  
Though the night conceal us.

**All:**  
**The Troth remains!**

**Presider:**  
Go without claiming virtue.

Go without surrendering judgment.

Go without making an idol of throne, pasture, fang, or fleece.

Hear the cry.

Bear what is yours to bear.

Return when you have failed.

Serve without needing to be called good.

And let whatever brightness is given  
answer only to Christ.

**People:**  
Amen.
