# The Short Rule of the Troth

## I. Christ

Christ is the Shepherd.

We belong to him.

The Order does not possess the Shepherd, the flock, or the Troth.

## II. The Troth

Before throne and pasture.  
Before fang and fleece.  
The Troth remains.

We do not claim the Troth as our possession.

We seek to remain faithful to it.

## III. Keeping

We keep what has been entrusted to us without possessing it.

Authority is responsibility, not ownership.

No guardian is made righteous by guarding.

## IV. Unclaimed Virtue

We do not claim virtue for ourselves.

We judge actions, name wrongdoing, and seek what is good, while remembering that our own righteousness is not ours to declare.

Christ is righteous.

We remain accountable.

## V. The Cry

We ask:

**What cry have I trained myself not to hear?**

We attend especially to those whom our loyalties, institutions, fears, and certainties have taught us to ignore.

## VI. The Path

We are capable of apostasy.

When we become false, we seek the Path of truth, confession, repentance, restitution, and renewed faithfulness.

No past faithfulness makes us incapable of falling.

No fall places a person beyond the Shepherd's call.

## VII. Mutual Keeping

No guardian keeps alone.

We accept correction.

We submit authority to accountability.

The greater the responsibility, the greater the obligation to be kept by others.

Even the keeper must be kept.

Nothing faithful keeps alone.

## VIII. The Throne

We shall not enthrone person, office, institution, nation, tradition, doctrine, or self.

Every throne remains beneath Christ.

The guardian must be willing to protect the community from the throne and the throne from itself.

## IX. The Fallen

We will seek truth without denying redemption.

Forgiveness does not require concealment.

Restoration does not always require return to authority.

For the fallen also:

**The Troth remains.**

## X. The Order

The Order is not the Church.

The Rule is not Scripture.

Aru Va'en is not revelation.

The Order exists to serve, and it may be corrected, reformed, or ended.

Its survival is never more important than its faithfulness.

## XI. Departure

No one is possessed by the Order.

A member may depart without becoming an enemy.

We release what we have no right to possess.

The Shepherd may lead where we cannot follow.

## XII. The Keeper's Promise

I will keep without possessing.

I will serve without claiming virtue.

I will listen for the cry.

I will accept being kept.

I will resist every false throne, including the throne within myself.

When I fall, I will seek the Path.

When another falls, I will not deny the possibility of redemption.

I will surrender what I can no longer keep faithfully.

I will remember that the Order belongs neither to me nor to itself.

Before throne and pasture.  
Before fang and fleece.

**The Troth remains.**
