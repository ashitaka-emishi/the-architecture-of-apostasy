# Apostolic and Companion Greetings

## Apostolic Greeting — user established

**Speaker 1:** *Before throne and pasture.*  
**Speaker 2:** *Before fang and fleece.*  
**Both:** ***The Troth remains!***

### Function

A mutual confession under judgment, not a password certifying membership or purity. Neither speaker utters the whole formula alone. Rank does not determine who speaks first.

## Greeting of Repentance — established working form

**Returning:** *I did not remain.*  
**Keeper:** *You are not the measure.*  
**Both:** ***The Troth remains.***

Repentance is not automatic absolution. Confession, restitution where possible, accountability, and changed practice follow.

## Greeting of Mourning — established working form

**One:** *The voice is silent.*  
**Other:** *The place stands empty.*  
**Both:** ***The Troth remains.***

The final line is spoken without triumph. Grief is not erased or converted into usefulness.

## Greeting of Departure — established working form

**One:** *Though the road divide us.*  
**Other:** *Though the night conceal us.*  
**Both:** ***The Troth remains!***

Distance and uncertainty do not alter the shared accountability.

## Greeting of Reconciliation — established working form

**One:** *What passed between us is known.*  
**Other:** *What was broken is not forgotten.*  
**Both:** ***Yet the Troth remains.***

Reconciliation does not falsify memory and does not require unsafe continuity.

## Greeting before Judgment — proposed

**One:** *No throne stands above it.*  
**Other:** *No fleece stands beyond it.*  
**Both:** ***The Troth remains.***

Keep this as proposed until explicitly approved.

## Liturgical rule

These greetings should never imply:
- “we possess the Troth”;
- “we are the righteous”;
- “the institution is the Troth”;
- “repentance erases consequence.”

Their repeated function is to place role, belonging, aggression, suffering, and self-conception beneath a prior fidelity whose Christian measure is Christ.
