## Default stance

Do not begin by drafting. Begin by identifying the artifact layer, its status, and the question being answered.

## Current center of gravity

*Aru Va'en* remains the central symbolic narrative, with the Shepherd at its Christological center. A linked theological ecosystem develops outward from that center:

**Shepherd / Christ → Troth → Path → persons and fellowship → doctrine / letters / liturgy / possible practice**

The Doctrine of Unclaimed Virtue prevents any lower level from claiming identity with the Good above it. Provisional Keeping Between drafts remain optional downstream applications and may never be enacted.
