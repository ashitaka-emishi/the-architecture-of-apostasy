# Open Questions

These are unresolved; Codex must not treat any proposed answer as settled.

1. After the first return begins, how should spoken confession, restitution, repaired trust, and any later responsibility unfold without collapsing them into reception?
2. Which surviving companions should become principal epistolary recipients rather than merely remain available?
3. How should the older phrase *Book of the Troth* be handled after D-095 establishes *The Book of Wolves* as the active collection title?
4. What is Vaerun's historical/literary location when writing letters: immediately after the Keeping, late in life, or across multiple periods?
5. How should apostolic authority be represented without reproducing a hidden hierarchy of moral possession?
6. What practices make Unclaimed Virtue falsifiable rather than a slogan?
7. What safeguards keep “hear the cry” from being manipulated by false or competing claims beyond the safeguards already established for the myth?
8. How do institutional repentance and personal repentance differ under the Troth?
9. How should the Troth material integrate with the broader Architecture of Apostasy / Architecture of Being taxonomy without becoming dependent on it?
10. What is the final redemptive formula parallel to “Even the keeper must be kept,” and should “Nothing faithful is merely carried” be retained or rejected?
11. Which fallen Lamb forms belong in the canonical mythology versus later commentary?
12. How should Christ-as-Lamb govern the Living Lamb image beyond the settled distinction between Christ and this corporate creaturely figure?
13. How do we prevent the Shepherd from becoming arbitrary divine command?
14. What Trinitarian claims, if any, should be made explicitly in the mythopoetic layer versus systematic commentary?
15. Is Hrovan best described as throne guardian, hall bearer, center guardian, or some combination?
16. What distinguishes a legitimate threshold from a boundary that has become idolatrous?
17. What criteria distinguish a faithful Vaerun crossing from transgression made into identity?
18. Is the Aruvan a singular mythic being, a class of emergent corporate beings, or both?
19. How literal should the “Long Wolf made by keeping” ontology become within the Architecture of Being taxonomy?
20. How does Aruvan relate to institutions, angels or principalities, corporate intelligence, and embodied symbolic order?
21. Beyond the established later correspondence from Little Vey, who are the principal named companions of Vaerun?
22. Where should the recovered Letter of Little Vey to the Vaerun appear in the collection, and how should Vaerun's response be preserved?
23. Which theological discoveries should Vaerun make during correspondence rather than know at the outset?
24. Where should Vaerun explicitly retract or revise earlier counsel?
25. What communal voices should close the collection after Vaerun's final relinquishment?
26. Should the provisional Sigil ever be adopted as a real practice of The Keeping Between, and what safeguards would permanently distinguish it from Vethra?
27. What conditions, if any, would show that The Keeping Between should become a provisional order, remain only a vocation/discipline, or pass away when its charge is complete?
