# Core Context

## The shortest formulation

**Speaker 1:** Before throne and pasture.  
**Speaker 2:** Before fang and fleece.  
**Both:** **The Troth remains!**

## Working conceptual architecture

### Project vocation
This project tracks the user's vision of the myth that is *Aru Va'en*: to understand it, place it in its proper place, and embody it faithfully. Embodiment may remain small, even an order of one, without becoming unreal; it must still remain under Christ, claim no virtue of its own, and possess neither the Troth nor the myth.

### The Keeping Between
The Keeping Between names a provisional embodied vocation for an age in which some structures are passing away. Its charge is to hear what must not be forgotten, guard what must not be surrendered, discern what is being born, and remain until the passage is complete. It claims no permanence, possesses no virtue of its own, and may pass away when its charge is complete.

### Aru Va'en and the Shepherd
*Aru Va'en* remains the project's central symbolic narrative. The Shepherd stands at its center as the explicitly Christological measure of every act of keeping. Doctrine, liturgy, letters, and provisional Keeping Between designs may unfold the myth's implications but may not displace its center or redefine its offices.

### Christ
The final Christian measure of the Good. The Troth must answer to Christ, not compete with him.

### The Troth
The Troth names **the fidelity of God disclosed and enacted in Jesus Christ, and the creaturely calling to participate in that fidelity by the Spirit**. It remains when human authorities, communities, moral identities, and self-conceptions fail, but it is not an independent law or second Logos and is not possessed by the Host or any institution. This is a constructive Christological formulation, not biblical lexicography.

### The Path
Movement from false logos / false embodiment toward truthful reordering and participation in Christ. The Path is not a badge already possessed.

### Unclaimed Virtue
A discipline against self-certification. Objective virtue is real; human beings do not become reliable measures of their own righteousness merely by believing themselves virtuous.

### The Cry
The suffering or demand of another that interrupts self-enclosure. A core diagnostic question is: **What cry have I trained myself not to hear?** The cry requires discernment; it is not a free-standing oracle above Christ.

### Throne
Authority, office, rule, institutional command. Necessary authority may exist, but it remains under judgment.

### Pasture
Belonging, enclosure, community, congregation, nation, fellowship, institutional identity. A pasture may shelter without becoming ultimate.

### Fang
Capacity for aggression, coercion, predation, or retaliatory identity. Naming evil does not authorize becoming what one opposes.

### Fleece
Vulnerability, innocence, victimhood, the identity of the wounded/protected. Suffering must be heard without granting moral infallibility.

## The central anti-idolatry grammar

- The throne is not the measure.
- The pasture is not the measure.
- The fang is not the measure.
- The fleece is not the measure.
- The individual is not the measure.
- The Host is not the measure.
- Vaerun is not the measure.
- Christ is the measure.
- The Troth remains.
