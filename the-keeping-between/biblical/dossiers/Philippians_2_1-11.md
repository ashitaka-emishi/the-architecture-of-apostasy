# Philippians 2:1–11 Dossier

## Project use

Primary biblical control for:
- non-grasping authority;
- status and self-emptying service;
- Unclaimed Virtue;
- the difference between obedience to God and institutional obedience;
- exaltation as given rather than seized.

## Constructive Troth synthesis

The sermon draft develops these claims:
- Christ's status is not exploited as possession;
- the throne does not authenticate itself;
- community/pasture is purified when it attends to the other rather than merely preserving itself;
- the fang is refused as identity;
- suffering/fleece is not itself moral sovereignty;
- the name is given, not seized;
- “let this mind be in you” turns Christology into communal practice.

## Exegetical cautions

Future research must treat as live questions:
- the syntax/translation of Philippians 2:6;
- `harpagmos` and “something to exploit/grasp”;
- the meaning of `ekenōsen` (emptied himself);
- whether the passage is pre-Pauline hymn material and what follows if so;
- the relation between Christ's obedience and human obedience;
- avoiding abusive uses of “self-emptying” to demand victimization.
